High Altitude Balloons

Launching High Altitude Research Payloads.

← Back to projects

Overview

Led the High Altitude Balloons (HAB) Team for the Columbia Space Initiative. Developed a complex payload of GPS, camera, and environmental sensors for data collection in the stratosphere. Created electronic diagrams and wiring schematics for electronic components. Key considerations: temperature regulation, reliable data collection, budget/funding, GPS location data for payload retrieval Designed custom protoboard with inserts for all electronics, combining environmental sensors with power systems, data logging, Arduino, and camera. Environmental sensors included temperature, UV, altitude, acceleration, and oxygen sensors. Tracking combined a custom built ameature radio tracker with a commercial SPOT GPS asset tracker.

Pico Balloons

Started program to launch Pico Balloons, small lightweight Mylar balloons designed for long-duration flights. Pico balloons are capable of circumnavigating the globe to collect data on temperature and wind. Developed a custom combination flight computer integrating GPS, environmental sensors, WSPR radio transmitter, and microcontroller. Flight computer to be powered by 6 micro solar cells, and would only transmit position and temperature during the day.

Impact & Results

Successfully launched and recovered a HAB in 2025. Retrieved video and Earth-facing images from the payload upon ascent. Imagery used by CSI and Columbia University for promotional material. Lessons learned from first HAB launch in 2024. Ameatur radio tracker and commerial GPS tracker intefered with each other, causing loss of location data until ameatur radio battery died. Additionally, due to high winds, the balloon was underfilled and did not have enough positive lift, leading it to land in the ocean. Successfully developed and manufactured custom flight computer for Pico Balloon. Launch TBD.
